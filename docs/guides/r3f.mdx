Fiber 9 requires React 19. Keep one copy of React, React DOM, Three.js and postprocessing in your app.

## Pass, not material

`CRT` processes the full composer rectangle. Place scene effects before tone mapping and keep CRT last. `<Canvas flat>` disables renderer tone mapping; the composer's `ToneMapping` supplies it. For an existing Canvas, move the mesh and composer inside it rather than creating another Canvas.

## Control preparation and reconstruction

`inputResolution="auto"` chooses a reduced input size; a positive integer sets the longest edge, capped at native resolution. `mode="pixelated"` shows prepared input and `mode="original"` bypasses the effect.

Update settings through props, such as `exposure={1.05}`. The optional `crtRef` in this example exposes the pass; after imperative updates in a demand-rendered scene, call Fiber's `invalidate()`. For animation, switch `frameloop` to `"always"` and update your scene with `useFrame`.
