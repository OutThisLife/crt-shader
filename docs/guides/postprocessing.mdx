## Do not swap the two CRTPass imports

The pmndrs composer needs `crt-shader/postprocessing`, not `crt-shader/three`. Keep renderer tone mapping disabled, add tone mapping in the composer, then CRT last. `ToneMappingMode.LINEAR` is neutral; choose another mode for your scene before CRT. Do not add Three's `OutputPass`.

CRT receives tone-mapped linear RGB and encodes screen output once. Offscreen output remains tone-mapped linear RGB, not scene-referred HDR.

For animation, call `composer.render(delta)` in your loop. Resize the renderer and composer together, updating the camera projection. `composer.dispose()` disposes its passes; your renderer and scene resources remain yours.

## Choose preparation separately

`inputResolution: 'auto'` reduces the input. A positive integer sets its longest edge, capped at the incoming buffer size. Use `mode: 'pixelated'` to inspect that input or `mode: 'original'` to bypass the effect. Keep the terminal pass enabled.
