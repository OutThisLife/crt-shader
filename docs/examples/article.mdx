## Source differences

The shader uses the Peach sprite from the [follow-up article](https://datagubbe.se/crt2/). Its palette and some pixels differ from the earlier image. The photograph also reflects the camera focus and video signal. The `reference` preset approximates that appearance; it does not simulate the monitor's full signal chain.
