## Article images

Game artwork and CRT photographs come from Datagubbe's [CRT article](https://datagubbe.se/crt/) and [Peach follow-up](https://datagubbe.se/crt2/). Copyright remains with their respective owners; redistribution permission has not been established for these archived images.

The Peach photograph is credited to [CRTpixels](https://x.com/CRTpixels/status/1400097860029489152). The follow-up identifies an S-Video source. The shader uses a separate Peach sprite whose palette and some pixels differ from the earlier comparison.
